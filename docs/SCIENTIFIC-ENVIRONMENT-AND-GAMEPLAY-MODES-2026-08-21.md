# qFoldIT Scientific Environment & Gameplay Modes

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Cross-engine capability contract alignment

## Scientific environment

Unity is aligned to `qfoldit.scientific-environment/1.0` for in-world scientific visualization:

- particles;
- fluids/liquids;
- volumetrics;
- scalar/vector fields;
- thermal/energy visualization;
- diffusion, dissolution and reaction effects;
- scientific, transparent, bio and reactive materials.

## Gameplay modes

The runtime should provide reusable templates for:

- Research Campaign;
- Arena Challenge;
- Co-op Lab;
- Sandbox Lab;
- Validation Duel;
- Build Mode;
- Racing Mode.

Build Mode maps construction mechanics to scientific constraints. Racing Mode maps route, timing, geometry or process optimization to a measurable mission objective.

## Quantum optimization

Mission sessions may use QOS-triggered, checkpoint, plateau-breaker, hybrid or player-assisted quantum scheduling. Every invocation is provenance-bearing and remains subordinate to scientific validation.

## Runtime boundary

Unity is an experience/runtime adapter. It may render and instrument scientific processes but does not define scientific truth.
